ingredients:
  - item: Brown lentils, rinsed
    quantity: 1½ cups (300 g)
  - item: Olive oil
    quantity: 3 tbsp
  - item: Yellow onion, diced
    quantity: 1 medium
  - item: Carrots, diced
    quantity: 2
  - item: Garlic cloves, minced
    quantity: 3
  - item: Ground cumin
    quantity: 1 tsp
  - item: Crushed tomatoes
    quantity: 400 g can
  - item: Vegetable stock
    quantity: 4 cups (1 litre)
  - item: Bay leaf
    quantity: 1
  - item: Salt and black pepper
    quantity: To taste
  - item: Lemon juice
    quantity: 1 tbsp
  - item: Fresh parsley, chopped
    quantity: Small handful
instructions:
  - Warm the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onion and
    carrots and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened — about
    6–8 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic and cumin and cook for one minute, until fragrant.
  - Add the lentils, crushed tomatoes, stock and bay leaf. Bring to a boil,
    then reduce to a gentle simmer.
  - Simmer uncovered for 35–45 minutes, stirring now and then, until the
    lentils are tender and the stew has thickened. Add a splash of water if
    it becomes too dense.
  - Season with salt and pepper. Remove the bay leaf.
  - Finish with lemon juice and parsley. Rest five minutes off the heat and
    serve warm, ideally with bread for dipping.
